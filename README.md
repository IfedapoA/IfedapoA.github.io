### Africa and Neocolonialism

This is a respository that covers the website that covers the issue of modern day imperialism and colonialism that is happening in Africa. This website will give awareness about the African continent - a group of 54 countries with different regions, nature, tribes, and languages. This mix scholarship article and news articles that will be appealing to different audience. This is to show that there is an unspoken Cold War happening between the West and the East in Africa, and a revamp of the scramble for Africa between the West and East in Africa. In my research, I aim to be without bias to any party - The West or the East. But I will be bias to the African continent since it is personal for me. In my information I wil be shifting between perspectives to give an hollistic view of things.
